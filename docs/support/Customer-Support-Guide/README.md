# Customer Support & Soft Skills Guide

Welcome to the Customer Support Guide. In IT Support, technical proficiency is only half the job; the other half is communication, empathy, and expectation management. This module outlines the standards for interacting with end-users and managing difficult situations.

## 🎯 The Philosophy of IT Support
- **You are supporting people, not just machines:** A broken laptop is a technical issue. A user who cannot present to a client in 5 minutes because of a broken laptop is a business crisis.
- **Perception is Reality:** If an issue takes 3 days to fix, but you update the user daily, they will feel supported. If it takes 1 day to fix, but you say nothing until the end, they will feel ignored.
- **Avoid the "Curse of Knowledge":** Remember that end-users do not know what DNS, DHCP, or Active Directory are. Explain things in business terms, not IT terms.
