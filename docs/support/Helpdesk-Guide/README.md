# Helpdesk Operations Guide

Welcome to the Helpdesk Operations Guide. The Helpdesk (or Service Desk) is the Single Point of Contact (SPOC) between the IT department and the end-users. This module covers the standard operating procedures for managing tickets within an ITSM (IT Service Management) platform like Jira Service Management, ServiceNow, or Zendesk.

## 🎯 Core ITSM Concepts
- **ITSM (IT Service Management):** The craft of implementing, managing, and delivering IT services to meet the needs of the organization.
- **SPOC (Single Point of Contact):** Users should not have to guess who to call (Network team vs. Database team). They contact the Helpdesk, and the Helpdesk routes the issue.
- **FCR (First Contact Resolution):** The percentage of tickets resolved during the user's first interaction with IT, without needing escalation. High FCR equals high user satisfaction.
