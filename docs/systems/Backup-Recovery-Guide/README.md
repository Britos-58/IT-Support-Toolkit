# Backup & Recovery Guide

Welcome to the Backup & Recovery Guide. In IT, hardware will eventually fail, and users will eventually delete critical files. A robust backup strategy is the ultimate safety net against data loss, hardware failure, and ransomware.

## 📂 Table of Contents
- [Backup Strategies](backup-strategies.md) - The 3-2-1 Rule, Full vs. Incremental backups.
- [Disaster Recovery Steps](disaster-recovery-steps.md) - RTO, RPO, and incident response for data loss.

## 🎯 Core Concepts
- **DR (Disaster Recovery):** The policies, tools, and procedures to enable the recovery or continuation of vital technology infrastructure following a natural or human-induced disaster.
- **Immutability:** Backups that cannot be altered or deleted, even by a Domain Administrator. This is the primary defense against Ransomware.
