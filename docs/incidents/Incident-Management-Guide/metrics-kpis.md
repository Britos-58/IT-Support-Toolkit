# Metrics & KPIs

To continuously improve our IT support and incident response, we track standard ITIL metrics. These Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) help management understand the health of the IT organization.

## 1. MTTA (Mean Time To Acknowledge)
- **Definition:** The average time it takes for a technician to see a ticket and begin working on it after it is generated by a user or monitoring system.
- **Goal:** Keep this as low as possible. A low MTTA shows the Service Desk is responsive.
- **Formula:** `Total time to acknowledge all incidents / Number of incidents`.

## 2. MTTR (Mean Time To Resolve)
- **Definition:** The average time it takes to fully resolve an incident from the moment it was reported.
- **Goal:** This is the primary metric of IT Support efficiency. Lower MTTR means less downtime for the business.
- **Formula:** `Total downtime / Number of incidents`.

## 3. First Contact Resolution (FCR) Rate
- **Definition:** The percentage of tickets that are resolved by the Tier 1 Helpdesk during the initial call or first interaction, without needing to be escalated.
- **Goal:** High FCR indicates a well-trained Tier 1 team and a robust Knowledge Base.

## 4. SLA Breach Rate
- **Definition:** The percentage of tickets that were not resolved within their designated priority timeframe (e.g., a P3 ticket taking 5 days instead of 4 hours).
- **Goal:** Maintain an SLA compliance rate of > 95%.
