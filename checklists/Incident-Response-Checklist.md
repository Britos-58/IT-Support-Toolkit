# Major Incident Response Checklist

**Purpose:** A quick-reference guide for the Incident Commander during a P1/P2 outage.

## 1. Declaration & Containment
- [ ] Declare the Major Incident in the ITSM tool.
- [ ] Open the War Room (Teams/Zoom/Slack channel).
- [ ] Assign roles: Incident Commander (IC), Scribe, and SMEs.
- [ ] Identify the scope of impact (Who is affected? What services?).

## 2. Communication
- [ ] Send the initial "Investigating" communication template to stakeholders.
- [ ] Update the internal IT Statuspage.
- [ ] Set a timer to send updates every 30-60 minutes, regardless of progress.

## 3. Mitigation
- [ ] Identify the root cause or a viable workaround.
- [ ] IC approves any destructive actions (e.g., server reboots, database rollbacks).
- [ ] Apply the fix and verify service restoration with monitoring tools.

## 4. Stand Down
- [ ] Send the "Resolved" communication template to stakeholders.
- [ ] Close the War Room.
- [ ] Schedule the Post-Incident Review (PIR) meeting within 48 hours.
